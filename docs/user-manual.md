# Hope HRS — User Manual
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-05-18

---

## 1. Registration

### Via Email
1. Go to the login page and click **Register**
2. Fill in First Name, Last Name, Username, Email, Password
3. Click **Register**
4. Wait for an admin to activate your account
5. Once activated, log in using your email and password

### Via Google
1. Click **Register with Google**
2. Select your Google account
3. Wait for an admin to activate your account

---

## 2. Login

### Via Email
1. Enter your email and password
2. Click **Sign In**
3. You will be redirected to the Employees page

### Via Google
1. Click **Sign in with Google**
2. Select your Google account
3. You will be redirected to the Employees page

> If your account is inactive, you will see an "Account Inactive" screen.
> Contact your administrator to restore access.

---

## 3. Employee Management

### Viewing Employees
- All users can view the employee list at **/employees**

### Adding an Employee (ADMIN/SUPERADMIN only)
1. Click **+ Add Employee**
2. Fill in the required fields
3. Click **Save**

### Editing an Employee (ADMIN/SUPERADMIN only)
1. Click **Edit** next to an employee
2. Update the fields
3. Click **Save**

### Deactivating an Employee (ADMIN/SUPERADMIN only)
1. Click **Deactivate** next to an active employee
2. Confirm the action
3. The employee will be marked INACTIVE

### Viewing Job History
1. Click **View** next to an employee
2. The employee detail page shows their full job history

---

## 4. Job Management

### Viewing Jobs
- All users can view the jobs list at **/jobs**

### Adding / Editing a Job (ADMIN/SUPERADMIN only)
1. Click **+ Add Job** or **Edit** next to a job
2. Fill in the details and click **Save**

---

## 5. Department Management

### Viewing Departments
- All users can view departments at **/departments**

### Adding / Editing a Department (ADMIN/SUPERADMIN only)
1. Click **+ Add Department** or **Edit** next to a department
2. Fill in the details and click **Save**

---

## 6. Reports

- Navigate to **/reports** from the sidebar
- Available reports:
  - **Headcount by Department** — number of active employees per department
  - **Salary Summary by Job** — min/max/avg salary per job
  - **Employee Full History** — complete job history for a selected employee

---

## 7. Admin — User Activation (ADMIN/SUPERADMIN only)

1. Navigate to **/admin** from the sidebar
2. The User Management table shows all registered users
3. Click **Activate** to grant a user access
4. Click **Deactivate** to remove a user's access
5. SUPERADMIN rows are protected and cannot be modified

---

## 8. Deleted Items (ADMIN/SUPERADMIN only)

- Navigate to **/deleted-items** from the sidebar
- Shows all INACTIVE records across Employees, Job History, Jobs, Departments
- Click **Recover** to restore a record
